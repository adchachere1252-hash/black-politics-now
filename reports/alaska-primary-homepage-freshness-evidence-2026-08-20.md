# Alaska Primary Evidence for Homepage Freshness Audit

**Retrieved:** August 20, 2026 (EDT)  
**Use:** Source package for a clearly labeled primary-context update. It must not be used to issue a general-election call, certify a result, or overwrite a general-election rating.

## Official preliminary return

Alaska’s Division of Elections published its August 18, 2026 **unofficial** primary summary at 11:59:51 AM on August 20. It reported 400 of 403 precincts, or 99.26%, in the U.S. Senate contest. Mary Peltola received 63,891 votes (48.09%) and incumbent Republican Dan Sullivan received 56,756 votes (42.72%). In the U.S. House contest, incumbent Republican Nick Begich received 60,125 votes (46.12%) and independent Bill Hill received 40,270 votes (30.89%).[1]

The same official summary reported, for the Governor/Lieutenant Governor top-four primary with 399 of 402 precincts (99.25%), Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins/Begich at 28,399 votes (21.60%), Tom Begich/Schuerch at 26,377 (20.06%), Bernadette Wilson/Shower at 13,714 (10.43%), and Dave Bronson/Church at 11,035 (8.39%).[1]

## Independent context and limitation

Alaska Public Media reported that the top four candidates in the governor’s nonpartisan blanket primary advance to November but that votes remained to be counted, with absentee ballots permitted to arrive until August 28 and certification planned for August 31.[2] Alaska Beacon independently reported the Senate and House leaders and explains that top-four candidates can withdraw before August 31, at which point later finishers may be promoted.[3]

Therefore, the public map should show a source-labeled **unofficial top-four primary context**. It should not label any Alaska federal or gubernatorial candidate a general-election nominee or change the general-election map’s rating/call status until the appropriate status is supported by final state information.
