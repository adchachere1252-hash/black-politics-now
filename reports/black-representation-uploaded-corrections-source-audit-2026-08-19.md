The South Carolina Daily Gazette reports that Alan Wilson won the June 23 Republican gubernatorial runoff over Pamela Evette with **68.6%** of about 320,000 votes, after Jermaine Johnson’s June 9 Democratic nomination.[7] This resolves the uploaded text’s internal inconsistency: Evette first led the initial Republican primary, but Wilson became the Republican nominee only after the runoff.

The Texas Tribune reports that Colin Allred defeated Julie Johnson in the Texas 33 Democratic runoff and will face Patrick Gillespie, who defeated John Sims in the Republican runoff.[8] This independently supports the existing TX-33 record’s office, winner, runner-up, result status, and general opponent. It does not support changing the existing 54.0% / 46.0% values without an official Texas historical-results export.

## Final Wisconsin and Texas 31 confirmation

The final Wisconsin result search resolved the user-supplied total discrepancy. NBC News and The New Yorker report **David Crowley 315,308 (39.8%)** and **Francesca Hong 311,512 (39.3%)**—a 3,796-vote margin, not the 3,783-vote margin in the uploaded text.[9] The platform uses the independently reported 311,512 total and the stated percentages.

NBC News’s Texas 31 result page projects **John Carter** as the Republican primary winner with **59.8%** and **Justin Early** as the Democratic primary winner with **57.6%**, against Stuart Whitlow at 42.4%.[10] The Texas Tribune results page reports Early’s exact Democratic primary total as **31,881 (57.6%)**.[11] This contradicts the uploaded text’s 37.1% figure. Because available direct sources did not establish Early’s Black Representation eligibility with the same clarity as the other candidates, the platform retains this as a reviewed candidate lead rather than creating an unsupported Black Representation profile.

## Candidate-removal safeguard

The Admin Black Representation workspace now has explicit one-at-a-time **Remove** controls for both profile and contest records. A removal requires a 12-character reason, may carry an evidence URL, stores a complete pre-removal snapshot and administrator identity in `candidate_removal_audit`, and cannot bulk-delete records. Profile removal is blocked when a linked contest exists, preventing a candidate profile from being removed while an associated public election record remains. Public callers cannot invoke either removal procedure.

The audit table was verified after deployment and contains **zero** rows: no candidate was removed to perform this correction pass. Candidate values embedded in shared Senate, House, and Governor race records remain evidence-controlled edits rather than deletions, so one action cannot silently erase a shared public race record.
