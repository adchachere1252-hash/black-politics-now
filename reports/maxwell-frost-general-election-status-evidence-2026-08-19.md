**Collected:** August 19, 2026 (EDT)

## Sources reviewed

Florida’s official 2026 General Election candidate listing identifies **Maxwell Alejandro Frost (DEM), incumbent, U.S. Representative District 10** as **Unopposed** in the status, primary, and general columns. The listing is the primary official evidence for the candidate’s general-election status.[1]

Florida Politics independently reported that Frost had secured another congressional term without a named primary or general-election opponent, noting that a prospective write-in candidate did not qualify.[2]

The initial statutory page reviewed, section 105.051, governs nonpartisan judicial and school-board contests and is **not** used as the legal basis for a federal congressional status. It is therefore excluded from the candidate-status evidence chain.

## Proposed platform treatment

The platform should distinguish **Won General Election — Unopposed** from a vote-counted or certified call. The record should retain the official Florida candidate-listing URL and the independent reporting URL, identify the general-election stage, and make the unopposed basis visible. This preserves the user-requested operational fact without misrepresenting the result as a counted election-night call.
