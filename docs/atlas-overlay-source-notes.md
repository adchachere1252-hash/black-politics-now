# Historical Atlas Comparison and Overlay Sources

The Historical Atlas national boundary geometry is supplied by the **UCLA Congressional District Shapefiles** project. Its documentation states that the collection provides digital congressional district boundaries through the 119th Congress and notes that the 2025 update includes version 2.0.0 extensions and corrections. The public Atlas labels this material as historical geography, not legal-map certification. Source: <https://cdmaps.polisci.ucla.edu/>.

Historical House member and party overlays use **Voteview** member data. Voteview documents the `congress`, `chamber`, `state_abbrev`, `district_code`, `party_code`, `bioname`, and `bioguide_id` fields used by the Atlas. Voteview identifies party code 100 as Democratic and 200 as Republican; the interface will not collapse other party codes into either major party. Sources: <https://voteview.com/data>, <https://voteview.com/articles/data_help_members>, and <https://voteview.com/articles/data_help_parties>.

The relevant public dataset citation is: Lewis, Jeffrey B., Keith Poole, Howard Rosenthal, Adam Boche, Aaron Rudkin, and Luke Sonnet (2026), *Voteview: Congressional Roll-Call Votes Database*, <https://voteview.com/>.

## Zambia World Elections Status Check — August 14, 2026

The Electoral Commission of Zambia’s public notices state that parliamentary and councillor polls took place on **Thursday, August 13, 2026**, between 06:00 and 18:00. Source: <https://www.elections.org.zm/>.

Reuters reported on August 14, 2026 that vote counting had been suspended after attacks on election officials and that the electoral commission had not announced official results, although preliminary constituency counts had been released. Source: <https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/zambia-suspends-vote-counting-over-violence-against-election-officials-2026-08-14/>.

The public platform should therefore label Zambia as **Results Pending / Counting**, not as an upcoming event, and should not claim an official winner until the Electoral Commission of Zambia announces one.
