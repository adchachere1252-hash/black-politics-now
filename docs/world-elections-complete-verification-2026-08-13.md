## Cook Islands — August 12, 2026

The Cook Islands general election remains correctly dated **August 12, 2026**. It is shown as **Voting Today**, not completed, because voting in Nassau was delayed to August 13 by weather and final results/government formation were not settled at the time of the audit. The page therefore shows no premature winner. Its country drawer provides verified candidates, four country-specific issues, and an explanation of the Nassau delay.[1] [2]
