# Black Politics Now News Mockup Comparison

## Decision

The recommended direction is **Original Style, Refined**. It is the only concept that directly follows the owner’s instruction to keep the existing Black Politics Now site recognizably close to what readers already know while introducing a small number of sophisticated improvements. It retains the newsroom’s familiar editorial flow and treats **John Lewis** as a permanent, respectful mission anchor rather than turning him into a large promotional device.

| Direction | Reporting hierarchy | Editorial categories and discovery | Fit with established BPN identity | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead Story | Strong primary-story focus | Useful, but shifts attention toward a feature-led magazine format | More of a visual departure | Not selected |
| Briefing Grid | Clear, modular presentation | Strong organization, but can feel like a dashboard | More product-like than the current newsroom | Not selected |
| Topic Rails | Good section-level navigation | High category visibility, but visually restructures the page | More of a digital-magazine reinvention | Not selected |
| Original Style, Elevated | Preserves compact story density | Improves grouping and discovery | Strong brand fit, but introduces a more substantial new visual treatment | Superseded by the more restrained direction |
| Original Style, Refined | Keeps the established lead-story, latest-headlines, and story-grid rhythm | Uses existing categories more deliberately without adding a new information architecture | Closest to the current BPN newsroom; maintains the John Lewis legacy panel | **Selected** |

## Recommended Implementation Scope

The future production News update should preserve the existing WordPress-backed article flow, site masthead, established navigation, and recognizable newsroom layout. It should apply only three visual improvements: more deliberate typography and spacing around the lead story, subtle warm-gold dividers and labels for hierarchy, and calmer thumbnail/card treatment that makes article discovery easier.

John Lewis should remain visible in a compact editorial legacy panel or similar persistent mission element. That treatment should be dignified and supportive of the journalism, never displacing current reporting or implying an endorsement. The Election Center, podcast experience, mobile layout, and the broader platform architecture are out of scope for this News-only refinement.

## Review Location

The selected visual is published for review at `/news-mockup` in the Black Politics Now platform. It is a concept preview only; the current live homepage has not been replaced.
