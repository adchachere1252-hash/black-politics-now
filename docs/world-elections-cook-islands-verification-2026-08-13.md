## Cook Islands — correct current status

The calendar’s **August 12, 2026** date is correct for the Cook Islands general election. It should not yet be presented as a completed, final result: weather delayed the Nassau ballot until **August 13**.[1] Preliminary reporting shows the Cook Islands Party (CIP) leading, but reports differ on its seat total and note that the final Nassau seat and post-election support arrangements matter for government formation.[2] [3]

> **Public-data treatment:** The record should show **Voting Today**, retain August 12 as the scheduled election date, clear any final winner field, and explain that preliminary CIP leads await completion of the Nassau vote and formal government formation.
