# Current News-Page Baseline

The current Black Politics Now homepage uses an article-led newsroom structure that should remain the foundation of the refined News page. It has a category-first navigation bar, a prominent lead story, four related headline links below the lead, a right rail containing Trending, Comments, and Latest tabs, a newsletter card, and a longer Latest News feed.

The current lead story is **“Supreme Court strikes down Louisiana’s second majority-Black district; state lawmakers approve map reducing Black representation.”** It is presented with a Supreme Court category, Black Politics Now byline, July 9, 2026 date, a featured image, a short introduction, and related redistricting headlines. Its full article page also uses related-story modules, visible share/view counters, an image caption, inline sections, and a Recommended For You area.

The News-only refinement should preserve this familiar reporting hierarchy while replacing weak visual rhythm with calmer dark-and-gold hierarchy, better thumbnail consistency, more intentional article cards, and two carefully sized editorial discovery modules: a **World Elections Map** slot and a **Historical Atlas: Selma** feature.

The existing Historical Atlas uses a dark editorial canvas, gold eyebrow labels, concise headline-and-deck treatment, compact fact cards, a state rail, and a focused history/detail panel. The Selma News-page feature should borrow that contained visual language as a compact discovery card—an Edmund Pettus Bridge or archival Selma image, a short historical context line, and a clear route into the full Historical Atlas—rather than attempting to reproduce the complete Atlas inside the newsroom.
