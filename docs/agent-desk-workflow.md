# Agent Desk Review Workflow

## Purpose

The **Autonomous Research Desk** continuously reviews Black Politics Now’s existing source-grounded platform context. It may identify coverage gaps, data-quality concerns, source-monitoring needs, editorial opportunities, and product improvements. It does not publish content, alter election data, or send public alerts.

## Human review path

| Stage | Who acts | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Recommendation | Research Desk | Creates a cited, review-only recommendation with a category and priority. |
| Assignment | Administrator | Assigns a named owner for follow-up without changing any public record. |
| Approval | Administrator | Approves, defers, or dismisses the recommendation. |
| Task creation | Administrator | Approval creates one private Agent Desk task, carrying the recommendation’s title, proposed action, evidence, and owner. |
| Completion | Assigned owner or administrator | Updates the task status; no publication or election-data mutation occurs automatically. |

## Election-night priority mode

An administrator may enable **Election-Night Priority Mode** for a bounded interval. During that window, an additional recurring review checks the platform every 30 minutes and limits its output to high-priority, review-only recommendations about verified race data, source coverage, reporting gaps, and public clarity. The mode expires automatically at the time selected by the administrator. Routine four-hour reviews remain in place.

> Priority mode is an editorial-review accelerator, not an autonomous election engine. DDHQ polling and official result workflows remain independently safeguarded, and no Agent Desk output can call a race or overwrite a public election record.
