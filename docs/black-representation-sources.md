## Editorial handling notes

- Source URLs are retained on every article-backed election record and exposed in the Election Center UI.
- Article values are preserved as reported. Any value that requires reconciliation with an official final canvass is annotated in the record notes rather than silently changed.
- The Black Representation roster includes all Black members and article-tracked Black candidates, regardless of party affiliation; it is not limited to formal Congressional Black Caucus membership.
