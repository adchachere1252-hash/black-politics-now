# 2026 Senate Verification Matrix — Results

**Completed:** August 11, 2026

The live Black Politics Now Senate dataset was compared against a 35-state verification matrix using Ballotpedia candidate-status coverage, Cook Political Report ratings, and the 270toWin presentation of current candidates and consensus ratings. State-level entries in the matrix include their own supporting URLs. See [the saved source record](./senate-verification-sources.md) for the method and primary nationwide references.

## Outcome

All **35 Senate contests** now have a reviewed candidate matchup, election stage, and current rating. The audit corrected the substantive records below while retaining pending-primary candidates as clearly labeled pending entries rather than treating them as general-election nominees. The post-fix reconciliation completed with **35 of 35** live records aligned to the verified matrix and **zero discrepancies**.

| State | Correction applied |
|---|---|
| Kansas | Updated Cook rating from **Solid R** to **Likely R** after the Democratic primary outcome. |
| Michigan | Replaced the prior Democratic-primary matchup with **Abdul El-Sayed (D) vs. Mike Rogers (R)**, retained Toss-up, and cleared primary-era reporting from the general-election card. |
| Montana | Updated to **Solid R** and preserved Seth Bodnar’s independent candidacy in the third-candidate field. |
| Nebraska | Replaced the Democratic placeholder with **Dan Osborn (I)**; updated rating to **Likely R**. |
| Oklahoma | Marked the Democratic contest as a **Primary Runoff** while retaining Kevin Hern as the Republican nominee. |
| Oregon | Corrected the Republican nominee display to **David Brock Smith**. |
| South Carolina | Marked the special election as **Primary**, updated to **Solid R**, and aligned Annie Andrews with the Democratic candidate position. |
| South Dakota | Replaced the former Democratic listing with **Brian Bengs (I) vs. Mike Rounds (R)**. |
| Florida, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Rhode Island, Wyoming | Marked current contests as **Primary** while their nomination processes remain unresolved. |

The following key matchups and ratings were additionally verified without needing a material data change: Alabama, Alaska, Arkansas, Colorado, Delaware, Georgia, Iowa, Idaho, Illinois, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Mississippi, North Carolina, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, Ohio, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, and West Virginia.

> The map’s rating colors convey the current Cook-style competitiveness classification. They do not declare a winner, and candidate-level primary results are kept separate from general-election reporting.
