# Election Guide Comparison Notes

**Source consulted:** [IFES Election Guide](https://www.electionguide.org/) on August 14, 2026.

Election Guide describes itself as an IFES database of upcoming nationwide elections and referendums, with systems, parties, candidates, key issues, and historic national-election information since 1998. It records both **declared** and **tentative** election starts and cautions that IFES does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of all information beyond its verification process.

## Platform comparison findings

The comparison used the platform’s records dated June 1 through October 15, 2026 and the Election Guide homepage entries captured above. It found that the platform’s existing calendar aligns on the core date for Algeria, Colombia, Kazakhstan, Morocco, Slovakia, Sweden, Switzerland’s June and September referenda, and the Cook Islands and Zambia election dates. The latter two require a **status review**, not an automatic correction: Election Guide labels them recently held, while their platform records still displayed `Voting Today` on August 14.

| Comparison category | Finding | Action taken |
|---|---|---|
| Date and event alignment | The platform aligns with Election Guide on ten represented event dates, including Kazakhstan (August 23), Morocco (September 23), Sweden (September 13), Slovakia (July 4), and both Swiss referendum dates. | No public change. Existing source links remain in place. |
| Recently held status | Cook Islands (August 12) and Zambia (August 13) remain `Voting Today` in the platform after Election Guide placed them in recently held elections. | Created a **high-priority, review-only** Data Desk item. The Cook Islands monitor remains review-only; no result or status was published automatically. |
| Calendar coverage | Election Guide lists Iceland (August 29 referendum), Russian Federation (September 18 Federal Duma), Sao Tome and Principe (July 19 presidency and September 27 National Assembly), and Latvia (October 3 parliament) where the current curated platform calendar has no corresponding record. | Created a medium-priority coverage review item requiring corroboration from official or high-quality sources before record creation. |
| Possible separate events or confidence differences | Election Guide lists a tentative Guinea-Bissau referendum (August 30) and Armenian referendum (June 30); the platform contains a December 6 Guinea-Bissau general election and a separate June 7 Armenian parliamentary election. Election Guide marks Switzerland’s September 27 referendum tentative, while the platform marks it confirmed. | Created a medium-priority source-watch item. These may be separate elections or a source-confidence difference; the public calendar was not overwritten. |

### Editorial conclusion

The World Elections map is **substantively aligned** on the principal represented dates in this benchmark, but it is not yet a full mirror of Election Guide’s broader 2026 calendar. This is appropriate only if the platform continues to label itself as a **curated, source-audited election intelligence calendar** rather than an exhaustive global election database. Three review-only Data Desk recommendations were created under Agent Run `450001`; none can mutate a public country record or alert readers without an administrator’s approval.

> Election Guide itself warns that IFES does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of all content. It is being used here as a valuable external benchmark, alongside—not in place of—official electoral authorities and the platform’s existing linked sources. [1]
